It’s been more than a few years since my last post in 2018! In that time, I’ve worked on a variety of software projects—from full-stack web applications to fintech systems—and learned a lot about coding, problem-solving, and collaborating across teams.

Looking back at my early posts, I see how much I’ve grown as a developer. While the fundamentals remain important, the technologies, best practices, and workflows have evolved significantly. In this revived blog, I plan to share insights from real-world projects, lessons learned, and tips for anyone navigating the world of software development today.

To start, here’s one key lesson I’ve learned recently: complex problems are often best solved by breaking them into smaller, manageable pieces and iterating quickly. Whether it’s designing a web application, debugging a tricky bug, or optimizing a system, this approach keeps the work structured and less overwhelming.

I’m excited to continue this blog journey and share what I’m learning along the way. Stay tuned for posts on new tools, project experiences, and practical tips for developers at all levels.
